# Glossary

**Post** — A single blog article written in Markdown, in English, made of text and pictures. Appears on the home page list, newest first.

**Draft** — A Post that lives in the repo but is not published on the Site.

**About page** — The single standalone page introducing the author, with a short bio and social links. It is not a Post.

**Site** — The public blog at `https://dylan-kim.github.io/`.
